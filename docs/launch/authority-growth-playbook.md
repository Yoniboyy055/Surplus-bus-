# Authority-First Growth Playbook (Disciplined Scale)

## Positioning
Surplus Bus should present as a calm, modern SaaS intelligence layer for serious operators.

## Sequencing Rules
1. **Signal quality first**: scoring relevance, ingestion reliability, transparent score breakdown.
2. **Trust velocity second**: weekly contextual intelligence, consistent insight cadence.
3. **Network effects third**: verified professional community after quality and trust are stable.

## Entry Funnel
- Primary CTA: **Get Weekly Intelligence**
- Secondary path: dashboard access and personalized ranking once users verify preferences.

## Psychological Principles (Ethical)
- Identity reinforcement: “built for serious operators.”
- Advantage clarity: show score deltas and relevance fit.
- Controlled scarcity: private features unlocked by plan/verification, never fake urgency.

## Expansion Discipline
- Build nationwide technical capability, but roll out GTM province-by-province.
- Move to broader expansion only after quality, retention, and engagement targets are met.
